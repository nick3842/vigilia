*vigilia.txt*  A dark theme: candlelight over a northern night

VIGILIA                                                    *vigilia*

Requires Neovim 0.9 or newer. No plugin dependencies.

USAGE                                                      *vigilia-usage*
>
    :colorscheme vigilia
<
CONFIGURATION                                              *vigilia-setup*

Call setup before loading the colorscheme:
>
    require("vigilia").setup({
      transparent = false,
      italic_comments = true,
    })
    vim.cmd.colorscheme("vigilia")
<
transparent         Remove editor, gutter, float and sidebar backgrounds.
                    Menus and highlighted surfaces keep their backgrounds.
italic_comments     Render comments in italics.

Each setup call resets omitted options to their defaults. Reload the
colorscheme after changing options. setup() alone does not apply highlights.

PALETTE                                                    *vigilia-palette*
>
    local palette = require("vigilia").palette
<
INTEGRATIONS                                               *vigilia-integrations*

Treesitter, LSP semantic tokens and diagnostics, GitSigns, Telescope,
Neo-tree, nvim-tree, nvim-cmp, and which-key highlight groups are included.
Integrations do not require additional configuration.
